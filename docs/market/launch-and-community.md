# Launch and community

Bonus prizes include a community vote and most viral (per the PRD, unverified until Official Rules). The plan is three short Taglish clips from demo footage, one post per day during judging, shared first in the builder's own communities.

## Key facts

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Clips | 3 vertical, 15 to 30 s |
| Captions | Taglish on screen with English subtitles |
| Channels | Facebook, TikTok, Instagram Reels, X, LinkedIn |
| Timing | One clip per day Nov 5 to 8 (Build Guide) |
| Voting rules | Follow the Official Rules. No vote buying, no fake accounts (Q2) |

## Clips

| Clip | Moment | Hook line (Taglish) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mug becomes kubo | Proposed: "Yung mug mo, bahay kubo na." |
| 2 | Tikbalang shove | Proposed: "Tinulak ng tikbalang si Spearman!" (from copy `tik.push`) |
| 3 | Sunrise replay | Proposed: "Hanggang umaga, kapit lang!" (from line bank) |

Proposed hook lines need playtester review like all Taglish copy.

## Audience order

| Order | Audience | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | College barkadas and student orgs in General Santos and nearby campuses | Builder's own world, Build for Your World theme | Assumption |
| 2 | Filipino developer communities the builder belongs to | Likely to try a build | Assumption |
| 3 | Metro Manila board game communities and events (All aBOARD, Mimic Manila) | Active tabletop scene | `market-and-audience.md` S12, S13 |
| 4 | Filipino folklore fans (Trese audience) | Proven theme appeal | `market-and-audience.md` S14 |
| 5 | Diaspora groups | Folklore from home | Assumption |

## Calls to action

- Ask people to try it, not just like it (PRD).
- Link the repo and the video.
- Credit friends who appear only with their written OK.

## Rules

- Follow any voting rules once published.
- No paid promotion unless the rules allow it.
- Real footage only.
- Track post links and results in `docs/playtests.md` or a new launch log (Proposed).

## Post-hackathon roadmap (for "what next" posts)

Regional folklore pack, classroom mode, phone as camera, wave editor, Glimmer recap on by default with a bundled local model (built but optional today), more languages if the voice model adds them. Out of scope for v1 (`product/scope-and-cuts.md`).
