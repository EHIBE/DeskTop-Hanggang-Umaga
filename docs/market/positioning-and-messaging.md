# Positioning and messaging

DeskTop is a shared-table game that uses what is already on the desk, in the language friends already speak, with stories they grew up with. The message leads with people and play, then shows the models doing the hard parts.

## Key facts

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Tagline | DeskTop: any desk is a game board. |
| Category | Co-op tabletop game played with real objects and voice |
| White space | The combination: real objects, Taglish voice, local folklore, no extra hardware, one table (`competitive-landscape.md`) |
| Avoid | "First" or "only" claims. No measured market claim exists |

## Positioning statement

For barkadas, student orgs and Filipino families who sit together but play apart, DeskTop: Hanggang Umaga is a co-op tabletop game that turns the mugs, coins and pens already on the desk into pixel art defenders against Filipino folklore. Unlike board games, AR kits or party games played on phones, it needs only a laptop, a webcam and an A4 sheet, and players give orders out loud in Taglish.

## Value propositions

| Audience | Promise | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Barkada (Migs) | A 15 minute game for the whole group with nothing to buy | Objects become units, 8-turn night, about $0.50 per night (estimate) |
| Facilitator (Ma'am Joy) | A safe, local icebreaker with a clear win or lose | Spooky not gory, Taglish plus English captions, 15 minute session |
| First-timer (Bea) | Learn by playing in one minute | Turn 1 has one aswang, tap controls, orders shown before they happen |
| Diaspora family | Folklore from home at the kitchen table | Glossary explains each creature, English works |
| Judge | Four models doing work chat AI cannot | Spark to SAM box prompts, SAM video reel, Muse Image style lock, Taglish diarization |

## Proof points to show, not tell

1. A real mug becoming the bahay kubo on camera.
2. A Taglish order appearing as a chip, then resolving.
3. A tikbalang shove that makes a player slide a real object.
4. The replay reel of real hands with the pixel battle pinned on top.
5. Eval numbers and cost per night from real runs.

## Tone

- Warm, playful, a little spooky. Like friends at a sleepover telling stories.
- Taglish in game and social copy, plain English in judge-facing text.
- Respectful of folklore, never mocking.

## Words

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Barkada, table, desk, real objects, together, until morning | Revolutionary, seamless, next-gen, unleash |
| "Built for my world", General Santos City | Claims about Filipino gamers that have no source |
| Model names with what each does | "AI-powered" without saying how |
| Spooky, cute, folklore | Horror, gore, scary monsters from a named place |

## Messaging rules

- Every number is sourced or labeled as an estimate.
- The player quote in the PRD press release is fictional. Never present it as real.
- The builder quote is a draft until EHIBE approves it.
- Hardware needs are stated honestly (laptop ownership is the weak point, `market-and-audience.md`).
